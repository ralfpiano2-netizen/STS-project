Guidelines

Collection and handling guidelines

A proposed workflow from household to recycler. The recycler partner has the final say on accepted grades, so confirm each rule with that partner before the pilot starts.

What to accept and what to refuse

MaterialDecisionCondition
Empty poly mailersAcceptClean and dry; remove paper labels and tape where possible
Bubble wrapAcceptClean and dry; free of paper and foam
Stretch and protective filmAcceptClean and dry; shaken out and loosely folded
Air pillowsAcceptDeflated, clean and dry
Foil-lined or metallized pouchesHoldRefuse until the recycler confirms it takes them
Wet, greasy or food-contaminated filmRefuseContamination lowers the whole batch; send to residual waste
Film with chemical or medical residueRefuseSafety risk for workers
Paper, cardboard and foamRedirectPlace in the matching recyclable or residual stream

Workflow, household to recycler

  1. Segregate at sourceEmpty the packaging and keep film apart from other waste.
  2. Check and cleanRemove paper, tape and labels; wet or greasy film goes to residual waste.
  3. Keep dryStore in a clean bag or lidded bin until drop-off.
  4. Drop off and inspectStaff check each drop against the accept and refuse table.
  5. Weigh and logGive each batch an ID; record kilograms and drop-off point.
  6. Bundle and storeTie into labeled sacks; keep covered, dry and away from heat and flames.
  7. Hand overDeliver with the batch ID; record the receipt, accepted kg and rejected kg.
  8. ReportUpdate the tracker and review the four pilot indicators.

Safety and worker protection

  • Never burn film, in the open or in drums.
  • Sorters wear gloves and closed shoes and wash hands after each session.
  • Film is flammable: keep storage away from stoves, wiring and direct sun, with water or an extinguisher nearby.
  • Keep children away from sorting and storage areas.
  • Give workers a written agreement stating pay and duties.

Who does what

Households and sellersSeparate clean, dry film and bring it to the drop-off point
Hub staffInspect, weigh, log, bundle, store and hand over
Local governmentSite, support staff and transport where agreed; collect refused film
ProducersFund collection, sorting and verification
RecyclerState accepted grades, issue receipts, report accepted and rejected kg

Is the pilot ready to start?

The pilot starts only when all six requirements are verified with written evidence. Tick requirements 1 to 4 once their evidence is in hand. Requirements 5 and 6 verify themselves from your batch log and your cost assessment. One open item means no-go.
